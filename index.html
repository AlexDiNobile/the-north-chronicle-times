The First Politi-Pal to Become Political Party Leader Days After Impeachment

The Free Advancement Party Becomes the First Political Party to Adopt a Politi-Pal Following Impeachment of President Richard Moore

By Elizabeth Jade

Elizabeth is a two time Pulitzer Prize winner for journalism.

March 25, 2037

In a stunning turn of events, the Free Advancement Party confirms that it will become the first political party to replace its party leader with a Politi-Pal. This news comes just days after the conclusion of the impeachment trial in which President Richard Moore was convicted and ordered to be removed from office. Within hours of the trial, news broke that the Politi-Pal would replace the outgoing President as the party leader. However, Vice President Joanne Maxwell will take office until the next election.

Politi-Pals are EvoTelligence's latest advancement in AI virtual influencers. In November, EvoTelligence unveiled the Politi-Pal at the World Tech Summit to the companies and investors in attendance. The summit was a closed, invite-only event, and press were not permitted to attend. However, after EvoTelligence was slotted to present, their stock price rose 35%.

Over the years, EvoTelligence has built a network of AI virtual influencers, many of which have gained significant followings over the decade. As their technology advanced and became stiff competition for traditional influencers, EvoTelligence turned its attention to politics, aiming to create “the most influential virtual influencer to exist,” according to insiders.

EvoTelligence's technological development in AI virtual influencers has advanced to the point that many of the issues surrounding them have largely been removed. The company has pushed the appearance and behaviours of its models to the point that consumers can no longer tell the difference between them and actual humans. While this was a massive step for EvoTelligence when it happened, there were still issues surrounding perceived authenticity, credibility, and parasocial interaction, as their AI virtual influencers were unable to achieve these traits due to consumers’ knowledge of their non-human status. However, given EvoTelligence's confidence at the summit when they announced Politi-Pals, it seems likely that they have advanced beyond these issues. Alternatively, according to AI-related research from Toronto Metropolitan University, the influx of AI virtual influencers by EvoTelligence in recent years may have desensitized the public to their presence, and the Politi-Pal creators may be banking on that.

According to a short leaked video of CEO Amanda Richwoman from EvoTelligence’s tech demo at the World Tech Summit, Politi-Pals aims to:

“...remove the human element from party leadership and replace it with something with a ‘perfect’ image.”  

EvoTelligence also promotes Politi-Pals as being accessible to anyone, anywhere, at any time, which gives them an advantage over traditional human candidates. This constant access makes them more likable and can have a persuasive effect on people.

Additionally, EvoTelligence states that Politi-Pals will collect data on the types of questions they are asked and the demographics of the people asking them, stating that the data is then provided “to the party for use however they desire.” There has been some pushback over the release of this information, as data privacy has been a major topic of discussion in government and among citizens after a string of data breaches across several companies and the protests that followed worldwide.

North Chronicle Times field reporters have been interacting with the public and have heard mixed reactions to the events, but a common theme, in the words of one person spoken to, is “Well, it can’t be any worse than what we already had.” However, there is also concern about political leaders being owned and controlled by a single organization, and what they could do with the information they have access to or what they could do if enough Politi-Pals enter party leadership positions.

While the public appears skeptical about the shift from a human candidate to an AI virtual candidate, EvoTelligence seems confident, based on its market research, that the transition will succeed.

Only time will tell how this will affect the future of politics.

President Richard Moore was contacted, but refused to comment on the conclusion of his impeachment trial, questionable history and past actions, the Nanogate Scandal, or his replacement with a Politi-Pal.
